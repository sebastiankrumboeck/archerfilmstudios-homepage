About the club

Good work starts with better company.

Frame & Motion is a small, generous club for people who make images and films for a living, for the love of it, or because they cannot stop noticing light.

We meet monthly to compare work, trade methods, and make something outside our usual circles. Bring a question, a half-finished sequence, or just your curiosity.
